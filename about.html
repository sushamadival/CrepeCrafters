Since 2021

The Journey of CrepeCrafters

CrepeCrafters began with a simple craving: the irresistible scent of golden butter, vanilla bean batter, and chocolate swirls wafting through cobblestone streets in Montmartre. We wanted to bring that exact Parisian crêperie experience to our college town and neighborhood.

Today, CrepeCrafters is a vibrant community meeting ground. We blend traditional French spinning techniques with innovative global flavors — offering everything from pure Belgian chocolate indulgences to savory garden herbs, creamy mushrooms, and tandoori paneer wraps.

Our Mission

"To spread everyday joy by serving artfully folded crepes crafted from genuine, wholesome ingredients in an inviting, stylish café space."
