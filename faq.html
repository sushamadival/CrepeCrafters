We honor traditional French technique! Our batter is rested for a full 12 hours so the flour fully hydrates, resulting in an exceptionally tender, lacy texture. We spin each crepe to order across genuine 400mm cast-iron bilig griddles using Beechwood T-shaped spreaders (rozell), folded hot and served immediately.

Yes, absolutely! We offer authentic Brittany-style 100% pure buckwheat batter (galette) which is naturally gluten-free. For our vegan and dairy-free guests, we have almond and oat milk crepe bases, plant-based mozzarella, vegan chocolate drizzles, and dairy-free whipped topping upon request.

Our online Custom Crepe builder allows you to choose your base batter (Classic or Chocolate), select any combination of fresh fruits, pick delicious chocolate or caramel sauces, and add toppings such as toasted almonds, Oreo crumbles, and vanilla gelato. The price updates dynamically on the screen as you modify your selections!
