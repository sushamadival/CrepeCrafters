Parking Information

  • Free Café Parking: 15 dedicated visitor parking stalls located directly behind our building off Artisan Alleyway.
  • Street Parking: 2-hour metered parking spots along Artisan Way (Free after 6:00 PM and all day Sunday).
  • Bicycle Racks: Secure sheltered bicycle and electric scooter locking stations positioned right by our front patio entrance.
🏛️

Nearby Landmarks

  • University Central Library: Just 300 meters west across the pedestrian skywalk.
  • Metro Transit Station: "Arts District Station" (Green & Blue Lines) Exit B is a short 3-minute stroll.
  • Campus Bookstore & Tech Hub: Located directly across the street on Artisan Way.
  • City Cultural Center & Park: 2 blocks south — perfect for grabbing takeout crepes for a picnic!
